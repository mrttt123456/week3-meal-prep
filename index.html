Carbs — go for

Rice (white, jasmine, short-grain, fried), udon, lo mein, toast, bagels, tortillas, potatoes, rice cakes, crackers, pancakes, fruit, dates.

Carbs — avoid

Quinoa, farro, barley, bulgur, lentils, beans or chickpeas as the main carb, grain salads, plain oatmeal.

Peruvian Lomo Saltado (Beef Stir-Fry) over Rice

Pack hot in an insulated thermos. Pack sauces, eggs and crunchy toppings in separate small containers so the rice does not go soggy.

PortionIngredient / preparation
6 ozLean sirloin strips (cooked)
2 tspOil for a very hot wok
2 tbspSauce: soy, red wine vinegar, 1 tsp aji amarillo paste, garlic, cumin
1/2Red onion, cut in thick wedges
1 cupTomato wedges + strips of yellow pepper
1 3/4 cupsSteamed white rice
To tasteChopped cilantro on top

Method Sear the beef in batches over high heat, add onion and pepper for 1 min, then tomato and sauce for 30 sec. Keep the tomatoes chunky. Serve over rice.

Gyudon — Japanese Beef & Onion Rice Bowl

Pack hot in an insulated thermos. Pack sauces, eggs and crunchy toppings in separate small containers so the rice does not go soggy.

PortionIngredient / preparation
6 ozThin-sliced lean beef / shaved sirloin (cooked)
1/2Onion, thinly sliced
1/4 cupSimmer sauce: 2 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp mirin, 1 tbsp sugar, 1/4 cup dashi or water
1 1/2 cupsSteamed short-grain or jasmine rice
1Soft-boiled egg, halved (pack separately)
1 cupSteamed greens (bok choy or spinach)
To tastePickled ginger, green onion, shichimi pepper

Method Simmer the onion in the sauce 3 min, add the beef and cook 2–3 min. Spoon beef, onion and some sauce over the rice.

Korean Beef Bulgogi Rice Bowl

Pack hot in an insulated thermos. Pack sauces, eggs and crunchy toppings in separate small containers so the rice does not go soggy.

PortionIngredient / preparation
6 ozThin-sliced lean sirloin / ribeye-style bulgogi beef (cooked) — marinate overnight
3 tbspBulgogi marinade: soy, brown sugar, grated Asian pear, garlic, ginger, black pepper
1 tspSesame oil (finish the beef)
1/2Onion, sliced, cooked with the beef
1 3/4 cupsSteamed white or short-grain rice
1 cupQuick-pickled cucumber + carrot (rice vinegar)
1/4 cupKimchi on the side
1 tspToasted sesame seeds + green onion

Method Sear the marinated beef and onion over high heat in batches until caramelized. Serve over rice with the pickles; gochujang on the side for heat.
